What it does

06 commands
"atlas"
The wake word. Atlas stays silent until you say it — no accidental activations from background chatter.
"open youtube"
Website launcher for common destinations — Google, YouTube, Instagram, and more, opened instantly in your browser.
"who is ada lovelace"
Wikipedia lookups, spoken back as a short summary. Handles disambiguation when a query could mean more than one thing.
"play [song name]"
Local music playback from your own library, matched by voice.
"tell me a joke"
A bit of built-in personality — jokes, identity questions, and casual back-and-forth.
"stop"
Ends the session cleanly, no dangling processes.

How it works

1

Wait for the wake word

Atlas listens quietly in a loop, checking short audio snippets against the activation phrase without sending anything anywhere.

2

Capture the command

Once activated, it opens a longer listening window and transcribes what you say using speech recognition.

3

Route the request

The transcribed text is matched against a command chain — site launching, Wikipedia, music, or a built-in response.

4

Speak the result

The response is converted to natural speech and played back, ready for the next command.
